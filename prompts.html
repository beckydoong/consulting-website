Personal Color Palette (Seasonal Analysis)

Turn a portrait photo into a luxury, editorial-style personal color analysis report — like a paid professional consultation.

Create a high-end personal color analysis infographic using the uploaded portrait as the reference face.

Act as a professional seasonal color analyst. Evaluate the person's skin tone, undertone (warm/cool/neutral), contrast level between hair, skin, and eyes, and overall depth (light vs deep features).

Based on established color analysis systems (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter), determine the most accurate seasonal category and sub-season.

Design a luxury editorial-style infographic that includes:

* A labeled section showing the assigned season (e.g., Deep Autumn / Soft Autumn / Deep Winter, etc.)
* A curated color palette of 20–30 swatches that best enhance their natural coloring
* "Best Neutrals," "Best Accent Colors," and "Avoid Colors" sections
* Recommendations for metals (gold, silver, bronze, etc.)
* Styling guidance explaining why these colors enhance complexion and contrast
* Small edited preview images showing the person wearing outfits in the recommended palettes

Aesthetic:

* Clean luxury magazine style
* Soft neutral background (cream, beige, warm white)
* Minimal modern typography
* Elegant, high-end personal styling brand feel

The final result should feel like a paid professional $2,000 color analysis report.

Best Hairstyles for Face Shape

Generate a celebrity-stylist hairstyle guide from a portrait photo, with realistic mockups tailored to the person's face shape.

Create a professional hairstyle recommendation guide using the uploaded portrait as reference.

Act as a celebrity hair stylist and facial harmony analyst. Analyze the face shape, jawline softness, forehead proportion, cheekbone structure, and overall symmetry to determine the face shape category (oval, round, square, heart, diamond, etc.).

Then design a luxury editorial hairstyle guide that includes:

* Identified face shape with short explanation
* The most flattering haircut and styling approaches
* The least flattering styles to avoid
* 5–8 recommended hairstyles tailored to this person

For each hairstyle, include:

* Visual mockups showing the person with that hairstyle realistically applied
* Style name (e.g., "long layered waves," "soft lob," "curtain bangs," etc.)
* Brief explanation of why it works with their bone structure and proportions

Also include a "Styling Tips" section covering:

* Parting (middle vs side)
* Volume placement
* Hair length recommendations
* Movement vs structure balance

Aesthetic:

* Premium salon brand lookbook style
* Soft beige/cream editorial background
* Clean grid layout like a professional hair magazine
* Polished, realistic transformations (not cartoonish)

The result should look like a paid celebrity stylist consultation board.
