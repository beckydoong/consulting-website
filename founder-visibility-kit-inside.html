Part 2 · The Founder Bio Formula

Your bio has one job.

People decide in about two seconds whether to follow you. A great bio tells the right person "this account is for you" — instantly.

I help [who] get [outcome] without [the thing they dread] · [one line of proof]

Examples

Skincare founder: Helping women 40+ get glowing skin without the 12-step routine · Formulated by an actual chemist 🧪
Fractional CFO: I help female founders know their numbers without the spreadsheet spiral · 9 exits and counting
Coach: Helping mom founders build 6-figure brands in 10 hrs/week · Did it twice myself

Rules that make it work

  • Name the person you serve. "Women 40+" beats "skincare lovers."
  • One outcome only. If your bio lists three, the reader remembers zero.
  • Keep proof to a single line. One number or credential is plenty.
  • End with a reason to tap the link — name what's waiting there ("Free visibility kit ↓").

✏️ Do it now: write your version of the formula, read it out loud, and cut every word you'd never say to a friend.

Part 3 · 20 Hooks Proven to Work Over and Over

Fill in the blanks. Sound like you.

These 20 formulas keep working because they match how people actually watch. Retention studies across millions of reels found "specific outcome" hooks hold roughly 45% of viewers, POV-style realism holds about 42%, and unpopular opinions hold about 38% — the three strongest patterns on the platform right now.

Two rules before you scroll: around 60% of viewers watch reels on mute, so put your hook on screen as a bold text overlay in the first half-second — 5 to 8 words, top third of the frame. Then check your retention chart: if 70% of viewers are still with you at second 3, your hook did its job.
1

"Stop scrolling if you're a founder who [specific struggle]."Direct call-out — filters for exactly your person in half a second.

2

"I did [specific action] every day for 30 days. Here's what happened."Specific-outcome hook — the top retention performer on the platform.

3

"How I went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]."Transformation arc — viewers stay to learn the how.

4

"3 signs you're [problem] — and what to do about each one."Diagnostic lists earn saves; saves tell the algorithm to push you.

5

"Unpopular opinion: [contrarian take on your niche]."Among the highest-retention openers; mild disagreement fuels comments.

6

"The biggest mistake I see [your audience] make with [topic]."Authority framing plus the viewer's fear of making it too.

7

"If I were starting my [business type] from zero in 2026, here's exactly what I'd do.""Exactly" promises a plan, and plans get saved.

8

"Things I wish I knew before [pricing my offer / launching / quitting my job]."Regret framing feels generous instead of preachy.

9

"POV: you're a founder and [hyper-relatable moment]."POV realism — the #2 retention formula, perfect for text-overlay reels.

10

"I tested [tool/strategy] for 30 days so you don't have to."You absorb the risk; the viewer gets the shortcut.

11

"Nobody talks about this part of [your industry]."Insider-secret framing — implies the viewer is getting the real story.

12

"Steal my exact [template/script/checklist] — it's free."Direct value hook. Pair it with your lead magnet link (see Part 6).

13

"This feels illegal to know."Pure curiosity spike — pay it off within 5 seconds or retention dies.

14

"Watch me [build / write / package the thing] in real time."Process content builds trust and works perfectly on mute.

15

"5 [tools/habits] I can't run my business without."Listicle + tools = the most shareable combo in the founder niche.

16

"Read this if you're building a business with [kids at home / a 9-to-5 / no team]."Constraint call-out — hyper-specific relatability beats broad appeal.

17

"Here's what [$500 / one hour] with me actually gets you."Transparency converts silent lurkers into buyers.

18

"The question I get asked most: [question]. My honest answer:""Honest" signals a real take is coming, with no pitch attached.

19

"Before you post another reel, do this."Interrupts the exact behavior your viewer is doing right now.

20

"Say this the next time a client asks for a discount."Word-for-word scripts are the most-saved format on Instagram.

Part 4 · The Trial Reels Playbook

Test your hooks on strangers first.

Trial Reels are Instagram's built-in testing lab. Flip the Trial toggle before you share, and the reel goes out only to people who don't follow you. Your followers never see the experiment. You'll need a professional account, and Instagram rolls the feature out to accounts with roughly 1,000+ followers.

This removes the scariest part of posting — looking messy in front of the audience you already earned. Every trial is a free audition in front of strangers, and strangers are exactly where growth comes from.

The playbook

1

Run 2–3 Trial Reels a week. More trials mean more data, more reach, and faster learning about what your audience responds to.

2

Change one variable at a time. Same video with two different hooks from Part 3, or same hook with two different trending sounds. One variable per test, or you'll never know what worked.

3

Read the results after ~24 hours. Instagram shows you views, watch time, and engagement from non-followers. Look for hooks that hold 70%+ of viewers through second 3 and pull new follows.

4

Share the winners. Turn on auto-share so strong trials post to your followers automatically, or share them manually once the data's in. Losers quietly disappear and cost you nothing.

5

Test adjacent topics. Curious whether your audience wants behind-the-scenes, pricing talk, or personal story content? Trial it. If a tangent hits, you've found a new growth lane. If it flops, nobody you know ever saw it.

🎬 Rule of thumb: when a Trial Reel wins, remake that hook into three more reels before you move on. Winners repeat.

Part 5 · The 7-Day Visibility Sprint

One small action a day. No skipping.

Momentum beats perfection. Each day takes 15–30 minutes.

Day 1

Rewrite your bio using the Founder Bio Formula. Update your link and profile photo while you're there.

Day 2

Post your story using Hook #3 or #8 — why you started, in under 150 words. Pin it.

Day 3

Engage before you create. Leave 10 genuine comments on accounts your ideal customer follows. Skip "love this!" and say something real.

Day 4

Share proof. A result, a review, a before/after, a milestone. Use Hook #2 or #17 to frame it.

Day 5

Teach one thing you know so well it feels obvious. That's exactly the content your audience saves. Have Trial Reels access? Run it as a trial with two different hooks.

Day 6

Go direct. Send 3 warm DMs — past customers, collaborators, or founders you admire. Open genuine conversations and save any pitch for later.

Day 7

Review + repeat. Check which post got the most reach and saves. Make next week's version of it. That's your rhythm now.

Part 6 · Start Collecting Emails Immediately

The algorithm rents you reach. Your list is yours.

Instagram can change the algorithm, restrict an account, or bury your reach overnight — and every follower you've earned lives on that rented ground. An email list moves the relationship onto ground you control. When you launch something, the sale happens in the inbox, where you reach 100% of your people every time you hit send.

The numbers make the case: email returns an average of $36 for every $1 spent — among the highest of any marketing channel. Welcome emails see open rates near 69% and drive roughly 3x more revenue per send than any other automated email. And speed compounds everything: people contacted within minutes of signing up convert dramatically better than people left waiting even an hour.

Start now, while your audience is small. A list of 200 warm subscribers who chose you will outsell 20,000 passive followers on launch day.

Your lead magnet: one problem, one fast win

  • Solve one specific problem your ideal customer has this week — a checklist, a template, a formula. Aim for 10 minutes to value, and skip the 40-page ebook.
  • Name the outcome in the title. "The Founder Bio Formula" beats "My Free Guide."
  • Deliver it instantly and automatically. The signup-to-inbox gap should be minutes, never "I'll send it tonight."
  • Make it a natural first step toward your paid offer so the people who download it are the people who'll eventually buy.

Your welcome series: three emails, that's it

1

Instantly — deliver the goods. Send the lead magnet, tell them exactly what to do with it first, and ask one question they can reply to. Replies train inboxes to trust you.

2

Day 1 — tell your story. Who you are, why you do this, and the one belief that drives your work. Connection here is what earns opens forever after.

3

Day 3 — help them win, then point the way. Push them to actually use the freebie, share your best related tip, and name the next step for whoever wants more.

Write like a person. One idea and one call-to-action per email — a welcome series with five links per email reads like a brochure and gets deleted like one.
